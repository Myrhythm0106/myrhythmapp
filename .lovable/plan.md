# Remembered, Reliable, Resilient

## How the three words fit together
- **Remembered** is about memory: what was said is kept.
- **Reliable** is about follow-through, which builds confidence: what I agreed gets done.
- **Resilient** is about bouncing back: a bad day doesn't undo everything.

Together they read as one story: MyRhythm **remembers** it, **follows it through** with me, and helps me **pick up again** when life gets in the way. Resilient is a better third word than Confident because confidence now sits inside Reliable. Each step I finish gives me more of it.
