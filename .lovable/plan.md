# Rehab-Ready Continuity Report (30 / 60 / 90) + a new plain-language definition

Two deliverables. One is a build, one is wording.

## 1 · The 30/60/90 continuity report

A single exportable PDF a rehab service can put in a patient file, generated by the member, covering three windows from a chosen start date (default: discharge date, else account creation).

**Route:** `/launch/continuity-report` — window selector (30 / 60 / 90 days), a live on-screen preview, and one Export PDF button. Reached from the existing Continuity page and from the Discharge Bridge page.

**What the report shows, in this order:**

1. **Header block** — member name, optional DOB, window (Day 1–30 / 1–60 / 1–90 with real dates), generated date, and the "Prepared by the patient. Not a clinical record." label.
2. **Commitments vs completion** — committed, completed, partially met, not met; completion rate; a small week-by-week bar so a clinician sees trend, not just a number.
3. **Goal Attainment Scaling-shaped table** — each goal with its status in GAS-familiar wording (met / partially met / not met), no numeric scoring.
4. **Support Circle involvement** — circle size, how many people were looped into actions, number of check-ins or comments received, and how many actions had at least one person in the loop.
5. **Discharge follow-through** — items originating from a discharge document or the Bridge Kit, and how many reached the calendar and were completed. Blank-state text when no discharge document was imported.
6. **Rhythm and engagement** — days active, capture and calibrate frequency, energy pattern.
7. **Boundary statement + the standard 3pt confidentiality footer.**

**Language:** behaviour and quality-of-life only — confidence, identity, behaviour, quality of life. No clinical outcome verbs, no scores, no improvement claims. Validated against `docs/claims-policy.md`.

**Empty windows:** if the member is only 40 days in, the 60- and 90-day tabs render honestly ("Day 41–60 not yet reached") rather than padding with zeros.

### Technical notes

- New `src/launch/continuity/buildContinuityReport.ts` — pure function taking a window in days plus rows from `daily_actions`, `goals`, `extracted_actions`, `support_circle_members`, `support_member_action_notes`, `document_import_audit`, `continuity_thread`, `growth_states`; returns a typed report object.
- New `src/launch/continuity/continuityReportPdf.ts` — jsPDF builder following the existing `continuitySummaryPdf.ts` pattern (orange header rule, teal section heads, 3pt footer with `EDITION_FOOTER`).
- New hook `useContinuityReport(windowDays)` for the queries; new page `src/pages/launch/LaunchContinuityReport.tsx`; register in `src/launch/routes.ts` (outer ring, not a headline door).
- No schema changes. Discharge-origin counts come from existing `document_import_audit` and the source fields already on `extracted_actions`.

## 2 · The new definition

The current internal term stays as-is. This adds a **public-facing one-liner** that anyone can hear and see themselves in, without naming the gap.

**Primary:**

> MyRhythm is the app that keeps your plan going after the appointment ends.

**Supporting sentence (used under it wherever the one-liner appears):**

> It captures what was agreed, turns it into daily actions that fit your energy, and lets the people around you help you carry it — so the plan lives in your week, not in a folder.

**Why it works for both audiences:** a survivor hears "after discharge"; a carer hears "after the review"; an ADHD adult hears "after the session"; anyone busy hears "after the meeting". The need is obvious, the gap is implied, and no cohort is excluded.

**Alternates to choose from:**

- "Plans are easy to make and hard to keep going. MyRhythm keeps them going."
- "The part that happens after the plan is made."
- "Where agreements become a week you can actually live."

**Where it goes, once you pick one:** landing hero subline, the App Store short description, the Rehab Partner Pack one-pager, and the founder outreach script. Recorded in `mem://brand/app-description` alongside the locked internal term — it sits beside Memory-First Design™, it does not replace it.
