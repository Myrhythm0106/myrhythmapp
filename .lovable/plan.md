## The exact tests

**Test 0 — Approvals (with me)**
1. In chat, type "send the card".
2. Approve both database changes when the cards appear.
Pass: I confirm both changes are applied.

**Test 1 — Signed-in walkthrough (your phone)**
1. Sign out completely. Go to myrhythmapp.com.
2. Tap Log In and sign in.
3. Check: the daily welcome appears with your name and today's #IChoose. Tap Start my day.
4. Go to the questions. Answer all 11.
5. Check: the snapshot opens with your MYRHYTHM score at the top, then the summary.
6. Tap "See the detail behind my summary". Check it opens. Tap again to close it.
7. Tap Keep these on your top 3.
8. In "Prove it now", save one real thing (for example "Call the GP Thursday").
9. Go to Home. Check: "My focus" shows your #1 area, and your saved item appears.
10. Close the app, reopen it, and check the item is still there.
Pass: every check is true, nothing freezes, and no screen confuses you.

**Test 2 — iPhone long recording**
1. On an iPhone, sign in and open Memory Bridge.
2. Tap record. Play a podcast or TV programme near the phone for at least 2 hours. The screen may lock.
3. Stop, then tap Save & Extract Actions.
4. Check: the length is right (within 1 minute), a write-up exists, and next steps are listed.
5. Fully close the app, reopen it, and check the recording and steps are still there.
Pass: all four checks are true. Note the phone model and iOS version.

**Test 3 — Android long recording**
Same as Test 2, on an Android phone in Chrome.

**Test 4 — Two-account Support Circle**
1. Create a second account with a different email (or use the tester account).
2. From your account, invite the second account to watch **one** capture only.
3. Sign in as the second account. Check: they see that one capture's summary and can send encouragement.
4. Check: they **cannot** see any other capture, transcript, audio or calendar item.
5. Back in your account, remove the permission.
6. As the second account, refresh. Check: the shared capture has gone.
Pass: the supporter never sees anything you didn't share. Any leak is a hard fail, so stop and tell me.

**Test 5 — Wording read-through**
1. I'll send you 30 #IChoose statements (one per month) and every 3-week plan line from the snapshot.
2. Mark each one Keep, Change (with your wording) or Remove.
Pass: every line is marked, and I apply your changes.

**Test 6 — Accuracy check (10 conversations)**
1. Run 10 real or realistic conversations through Memory Bridge: appointments, phone calls, family plans.
2. Score each extracted step: Accurate, Editable (right idea, small fix) or Wrong (invented, missed or wrong person).
3. Fill in the table: Total 10 | Accurate __ | Editable __ | Wrong __.
Pass: at least 8 of 10 are Accurate or Editable, and **no** invented step is shown as fact.

**Test 7 — Calendar handoff**
1. From a next step, tap Add to my calendar → Google. Check it appears in Google Calendar.
2. Repeat with Outlook.
3. On an iPhone, download the file. Check it opens in Apple Calendar.
Pass: all three land with the right date and time.

**Test 8 — Fresh eyes**
1. Give one trusted friend who has never seen the app the tester briefing, and nothing else.
2. Watch silently while they do it. Don't help.
3. Write down every pause, every question they ask, and every "oh".
Pass: they reach their snapshot and save one thing without your help.

## Gate decision (Day 12)
- All tests pass → send the briefing and form to the first 5 testers. Add 5 more a week later if nothing serious comes up.
- Any test fails → the founders group waits. I fix the problem and you redo that test only.
- Payments stay in test mode throughout.
