# Turn the MYRHYTHM report into a living “My Compass”

## Expert direction

The report should not sell by withholding the most important finding. It should reveal enough truth to make the user think: **“That feels like me — and I know what to do next.”** Membership then sells the ongoing help to put that insight into practice.

This is a stronger position than a static score, personality result or generic brain-training report. MyRhythm’s advantage is the continuing chain:

**My answers → My insight → My chosen action → My real schedule → My follow-through → My review**

The user remains the author throughout. MyRhythm reflects, suggests and remembers; it never defines the person or takes control away.

## 1. Be transparent before the assessment

Change “What to expect” so it plainly promises:

- “At the end, I’ll see my MYRHYTHM snapshot and one complete key insight.”
- “I’ll choose one action to try.”
- “The full personalized plan and ongoing follow-through are included with membership.”

The assessment remains optional, and the user can still go to Home without completing it.

## 2. Make the free report revealing and engaging

Keep the report template and visual structure the user loves. Rebuild its central story around five short sections:

1. **What I told MyRhythm** — reflect the user’s own primary and “also fits” answers in recognizable plain language.
2. **What this may mean for my days** — connect patterns across answers without diagnosing or presenting them as facts.
3. **My key insight right now** — identify the most useful pattern, explain why it matters, and cite the answers that shaped it.
4. **What is already working for me** — show one strength alongside the area needing attention, so the report is empowering rather than deficit-led.
5. **One action I choose now** — offer up to three relevant actions; the user chooses, edits or writes their own.

Use phrases such as “You told me…”, “This may mean…”, and “Does this feel right?” Do not use a blunt “softest facet” label without context.

## 3. Create a persistent, user-owned “My Compass”

After the report, save a concise personal compass containing:

- **What matters to me now**
- **What makes things harder for me**
- **What helps me**
- **When I tend to be at my best**
- **Who I want involved, if anyone**
- **What I am trying next**
- **When I want to review it**

Every item must preserve its origin:

- “From my assessment”
- “I added this”
- “Suggested from my recent activity”

The user can edit, accept, dismiss or replace every interpreted item. Their words take priority over generated wording.

## 4. Make the user’s perspective a constant

Apply a shared “user perspective” rule wherever MyRhythm makes a suggestion:

- draw first from the latest accepted My Compass;
- show a short **Why this?** explanation linked to the relevant user answer or choice;
- phrase suggestions in the first person where appropriate;
- never silently change the Compass because of app activity;
- ask permission before adopting a new pattern;
- allow **Not true for me**, **Change this**, and **Pause this**;
- keep historical versions so the user can see what changed and restore an earlier view.

This rule should inform scheduling, plans, reminders, Memory Bridge actions and Support Circle suggestions without adding extra steps to routine use.

## 5. Let the user view themselves whenever they choose

Make **My Compass** reachable from Home and the existing Brain Health area in one tap. It should support three simple views:

- **Now** — current Compass, current action and next review.
- **Then & now** — meaningful changes since the previous review, using the user’s own words before scores.
- **My story** — dated snapshots, answers, chosen actions and completed follow-through.

Keep the existing assessment history and trend view, but lead with human changes rather than a score graph alone.

## 6. Support planned reflection without nagging

At the end of the report, ask one question:

**“When would I like to look at this again?”**

Offer no more than three choices:

- In 7 days
- In 30 days
- Choose a date

Also include **Not now**. The chosen review appears in the app’s calendar/reminders and can be changed or cancelled. A review should first ask “What feels different?” before inviting a retake; it must not imply that a score change is a medical outcome.

## 7. Make the membership choice fully transparent

After the free insight and chosen action, show a clear split:

**Included now, free**
- Full snapshot and eight-part picture
- One complete key insight with its supporting answers
- One strength and one action chosen by the user
- Current best-time suggestion
- Ability to revisit this snapshot

**Membership helps me put it into practice**
- Complete personalized action plan
- Turn chosen actions into dates, reminders and calendar entries
- Use Memory Bridge to carry decisions forward
- Optional Support Circle follow-through
- Planned reviews, comparisons and adaptations over time

Show the real terms before the button:

- Founding Member: £10/month for life while the subscription remains active
- Regular price: £15/month
- 7-day free trial, card required, cancel before the trial ends to avoid being charged

Use **See membership options**, not language implying immediate payment. Keep **Continue with my free snapshot** clearly available.

## 8. SMART operating standard

Every chosen action created from the report must contain:

- **Specific:** one clear action in the user’s words;
- **Measurable:** a visible done state;
- **Achievable:** sized to the person’s stated energy and capacity;
- **Relevant:** linked to the key insight and the user’s stated priority;
- **Time-bound:** a chosen date or review point.

Present this as one simple sentence, not a form. Example:

> “On Tuesday at 10:00, I’ll spend 15 minutes writing my three questions before the appointment. I’ll know I’m done when they are saved in MyRhythm.”

The user can change any part before adding it to their day.

## Technical approach

- Extend the existing assessment result derivation with a deterministic insight/strength/action mapping based on saved primary and secondary answers. Avoid generative claims for the first release.
- Preserve the current assessment storage and report acknowledgment behaviour.
- Add a versioned My Compass record linked to the user and assessment run, with user-authored and suggested values distinguishable.
- Reuse existing action, calendar, reminder and assessment-history capabilities rather than creating a parallel workflow.
- Keep all personal records private by default under existing user-scoped access rules; Support Circle visibility remains explicitly permission-based.

## Verification

- Complete the assessment on a phone and confirm the user understands the free/paid split before answering.
- Confirm the free report reflects actual selected answers, identifies one strength and one insight, and offers relevant editable actions.
- Confirm every interpretation explains “why” and can be rejected or changed.
- Confirm a chosen action becomes a valid SMART action and can enter the calendar/reminder flow.
- Confirm the Compass is reachable in one tap, supports Now / Then & now / My story, and retains earlier versions.
- Confirm planned review reminders can be scheduled, changed and cancelled.
- Confirm price, trial, billing and cancellation terms appear before membership selection.
- Confirm the free route never traps or pressures the user.
- Confirm all wording is non-clinical, reassuring, first-person and understandable without remembering earlier screens.
