# Make the MYRHYTHM results offer transparent and worth paying for

## Expert recommendation

Do not hide the most useful insight and then reveal a paywall. That risks feeling like a bait-and-switch, especially for someone with memory or cognitive fatigue.

Use a **value-first report**:

- Give the user one complete, meaningful key insight free.
- Clearly label what their free snapshot includes.
- Show the paid personalized report as the next level: not “the answer withheld”, but a practical plan for acting on what they have learned.
- State the price and trial terms before the user presses the membership button.

This creates a stronger distinction from basic assessment and brain-training products: MyRhythm does not charge merely to reveal a score; membership turns insight into day-to-day follow-through.

## Changes

### 1. Set expectations before the questions

Update the “What to expect” screen to say, in plain language:

- “At the end, you’ll see your MYRHYTHM snapshot and one key insight.”
- “Your full personalized report and 3-step action plan are included with membership.”

Keep the assessment optional and retain “Not now — take me to my day.”

### 2. Make the free result genuinely useful

On the results page, replace the buried paywall teaser with a prominent **My key insight** section that shows:

- the facet that most needs attention;
- a short explanation of why it matters in everyday life;
- one practical first action the user can take today;
- the existing non-clinical snapshot disclaimer.

The insight must use the user’s actual answers and existing score data, not generic or alarming language.

### 3. Explain exactly what membership unlocks

Directly below the free insight, add a concise comparison:

**Included in my free snapshot**
- MYRHYTHM overview and eight-facet picture
- My key insight
- My suggested best time window
- One action I can try today

**Included with membership**
- My complete personalized report
- A tailored 3-step plan for the key insight
- Ongoing scheduling around my energy and focus
- Follow-through through Memory Bridge, reminders and optional Support Circle
- Progress and changes over time

Do not blur, obscure, or tease content the user cannot inspect.

### 4. Make the purchase choice fully transparent

Place the real offer beside the membership button:

- “Founding Member — £10/month for life while the subscription stays active”
- “Regular price £15/month”
- “7-day free trial; card required; cancel before the trial ends to avoid being charged”

Use the canonical pricing configuration so displayed terms cannot drift. The button should say **See membership options** rather than imply that payment happens immediately.

Add a quiet **Continue with my free snapshot** action that takes the user onward without pressure.

### 5. Keep the template the user loves

Preserve the current editorial report layout, charts, letter bars, best-window section, colours and overall visual hierarchy. Only restructure the insight and membership area for clarity.

## Verification

- Complete the assessment on a phone-size screen and confirm the free/paid distinction is stated before answering.
- Confirm the free key insight, explanation and one useful action are visible without paying.
- Confirm price, trial, billing and cancellation wording appears before the membership choice.
- Confirm “Continue with my free snapshot” works and the user is not trapped.
- Confirm membership navigation still reaches the existing payment screen.
- Confirm all wording remains non-clinical, reassuring and readable without relying on memory.
