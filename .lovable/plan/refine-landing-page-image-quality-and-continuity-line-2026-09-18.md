# Refine Landing-Page Image Quality and Continuity Line

## Goal
Make the photographic story feel premium and intentional, with no wording embedded over images and no connecting line appearing to cut through picture 01.

## Confirmed issue
- Picture 01 currently contains the visible overlay label “CONVERSATION OR REPORT” inside the photograph.
- The vertical continuity line meets the photograph in a way that reads as an accidental overlap rather than a polished connection.
- The chapter number also sits very close to the image edge, which weakens the finish.

## Changes
1. Remove the bottom overlay wording and coloured dot from all four photographs, keeping the images clean and editorial.
2. Move each short stage label into the adjacent text area, where it remains readable without covering a person or photograph.
3. Redesign the continuity line so it ends at a deliberate connector point before each photograph rather than visually passing into it.
4. Refine the numbered markers so they sit cleanly between the text and image without clipping at page edges.
5. Review all four images at desktop and mobile sizes for cropping, sharpness, realism, text overlap, and consistent presentation.
6. Preserve the approved photographs, wording, page order, colours, and single Founding Member action.
