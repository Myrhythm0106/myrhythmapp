# MyRhythm — earn readiness for a small private pilot

## Decision
Do not open unrestricted public or paid use yet. Finish the current Memory Bridge improvements, prove the essential journey, then run a small, invited, founder-supported pilot.

Compilation checks support continuing development; they do not establish reliable recording, privacy, notification delivery or usability for people with memory challenges.

## 1. Finish, then freeze the pilot experience
- Finish and check the approved guided Memory Bridge and calm transcript plans.
- Confirm short transcript sections, speaker names supplied by the user, accurate original-word links and the relevant calendar-day link.
- Complete recording-removal notices and verify the transcript survives audio expiry where requested. Keep explicit privacy choices intact.
- Fix failures found in these flows before adding more features.

## 2. Five gates before independent invited access
### Account and navigation
Run a real new-user journey from Join Now through account, questions, free snapshot and Home; then sign out and return. Confirm there is no loop or misleading destination.

### Capture and recovery
On a real Android phone and iPhone, record a short conversation, save, reopen, read, rename a speaker, check next steps and add them to the day. Exercise interruption and connection failure without losing the captured conversation. Test the intended pilot recording length; do not offer unproven 2–4-hour recording promises.

### Accuracy and visible outcome
Check a varied sample of conversations against the original words, including no actions and unclear speech. Review action wording, owner and dates; reject invented commitments. Confirm approved steps read back through the calendar and failures remain recoverable.

### Privacy and removal
Check private records across two real accounts. Verify grant, limited access and revocation before enabling Support Circle for testers. If this cannot be verified, keep sharing unavailable in the pilot. Verify audio removal, retained write-up and dated removal reference; do not use sensitive real-life recordings until these checks pass.

### Ease of use and reminders
Observe an older adult and someone with memory challenges using the core journey with consent and support available. Check whether they can start, save, find their result and return Home without coaching. Test the reminder channels actually offered; do not imply notifications arrive on a locked phone unless proven.

## 3. Run a discreet, useful pilot
- Start with 5–8 invited adults over 7–14 days, with clear early-test consent, no paid commitment and direct founder support.
- First sessions use non-sensitive practice conversations with everyone’s recording permission. Retain another copy of important information; no medical decisions, medication reliance or urgent-care use.
- Each tester repeats one practical task: conversation → checked next step → day → completion, and tries finding it again later.
- Track whether the task succeeds, help needed, confusing moments, saving failures and whether they choose to return. Separate opinions about appearance from evidence of useful outcomes.
- Pause access after any unexplained data exposure, lost capture or misleading scheduling result. Fix and retest before expanding.
- Proceed to a wider cohort only when essential gates pass and repeated sessions show useful, understandable outcomes.

## 4. Position the difference honestly
- Current public websites show Granola, Plaud and Otter offering combinations of capture, notes, actions and follow-ups. Do not claim these individual features are unique.
- Tiimo’s current product page targets flexible neurodivergent planning; BrainAid positions PEAT as provider-led cognitive support. MyRhythm should not adopt clinical claims or imply equivalent clinical evidence.
- Test MyRhythm’s proposed distinction: **checked, traceable next steps that fit everyday life, with permission-given support for follow-through**.
- Start with memory-challenge users as the primary design audience. Test stressed/busy adults as a secondary group rather than diluting the product into an all-purpose tool.
- Website observations are positioning evidence only; competitor usability, superiority, acquisition costs and conversion remain unverified.
