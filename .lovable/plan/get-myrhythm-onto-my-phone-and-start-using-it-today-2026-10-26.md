# Get MyRhythm onto my phone and start using it today

## Deliverable: "How to Get Set Up" guide (printable PDF)
A step-by-step guide covering Steps 2 to 5 below. Each step has a tick box, what I should see when it works, and "If it doesn't" help. It also has a notes line per step and a final "Set-up passed" box. It uses MyRhythm's colours and is easy to follow on paper or on the phone. It's saved to Files and downloadable from Help.

## Step 1: I publish the latest version (done for you on approval)
Today's changes (the new welcome, the simpler question screen, and watch and phone uploads) are only in the preview so far. Publishing puts them live at **myrhythmapp.com**. The security check is already clear.

## Step 2: You put MyRhythm on your phone (2 minutes, no app store)
**iPhone (Safari):**
1. Open **myrhythmapp.com** in Safari.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with an arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open MyRhythm from the new icon on your home screen.

**Android (Chrome):**
1. Open **myrhythmapp.com** in Chrome.
2. Tap the **three dots** menu, then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

## Step 3: Your first 10 minutes
1. Tap **Get Started** and create your account. Use a fresh email to see the new welcome as a newcomer would.
2. Choose **Guide me**.
3. Answer the quick questions (about 6 minutes) and see your snapshot.
4. Open **Memory Bridge**, tap **Record**, say a 30-second test ("I'll call the dentist on Friday"), then tap **Save and find my next steps**.
5. Tap **Put these in my day** and check that the step appears in your day.

## Step 4: Set up one-tap recording (optional, 3 minutes)
In **Settings, under "Tap to record"**, follow the steps. On iPhone, tapping the back of the phone three times starts a recording. On Android, it's a Record button on your home screen.

## Step 5: Start the testing plan
Download it from **Help, then "Download testing plan (PDF)"**. Begin with Test 1, the walkthrough on your phone, and note anything that makes you stop and think "what do I do here?".
