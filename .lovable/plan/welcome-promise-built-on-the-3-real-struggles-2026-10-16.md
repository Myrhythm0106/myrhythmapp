# Welcome promise built on the 3 real struggles

## The 3 things people with memory challenges or brain injury live with
1. **What was said is gone.** Conversations, appointments and instructions fade within hours.
2. **Things don't get done.** Plans start, then slip, so appointments and promises are missed.
3. **Confidence wears away.** Tiredness and overwhelm lead to second-guessing myself and leaning on others.

Each card names one struggle and answers it with a feeling the person wants back: **remembered**, **reliable**, **confident**. This echoes the landing page: "It remembers, so you don't have to."

## New copy

Heading: "Welcome, {name}. MyRhythm remembers, so I don't have to."
Intro: "This takes about 6 minutes. My answers set MyRhythm up around me."

1. **Remembered.**
   *When I forget what was said:* I record it, and MyRhythm keeps every word and pulls out every next step.

2. **Reliable.**
   *When things slip:* each step gets a time in my day and a reminder, and MyRhythm stays with it until it's done.

3. **Confident.**
   *When I doubt myself:* I can check what was agreed at any time. I decide, and nothing changes without my yes.

Under the cards, in bold teal: "My snapshot and first 3 weeks are free and mine to keep."
