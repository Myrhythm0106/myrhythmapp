# Readiness verdict: can MyRhythm go to an outside audience?

## Short answer
**Not to the general public yet. Yes to a small, briefed founders group (5–15 people) once the 5 items below are cleared.**

Overall readiness: **6.5 / 10** for public use. **8 / 10** for a hand-held founders group.

## Scores
| Area | Score | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Concept and story | 9/10 | Clear promise (Pocket PA, follow-through after the conversation ends), strong snapshot report, daily #IChoose welcome |
| Onboarding flow | 8/10 | One front door, clear steps, value before payment. Snapshot is still long |
| Core features (capture → steps → calendar) | 7/10 | Works end to end in testing; long recordings on real phones still unproven |
| Trust and safety | 5/10 | Two database security/save changes still awaiting approval; Support Circle permissions not tested across two real accounts; no outside security or accessibility review |

| Proof | 3/10 | No box on the Proof Checklist is ticked yet |

## What must happen before sending to anyone outside
1. **Approve the two pending database changes** (recording-time security fix; save and resume across devices).
2. **Real phone test** — a 1–2 hour recording on one iPhone and one Android, through to next steps and calendar.
3. **Two-account Support Circle test** — confirm a supporter sees only what was shared.
4. **Read-through of AI-written wording** — a sample of the 365 #IChoose statements and the 3-week plan lines on the snapshot.
5. **Signed-in walkthrough on your phone** — sign in, see the daily welcome, finish the questions, keep your top 3, save one real thing, check it on Home.

## Before going fully public (later)
- Switch payments to live.
- Pass the 20-conversation accuracy check (16 of 20 usable, nothing invented).
- Outside accessibility and security review.
- Clear disclaimer visible on sign-up (already in place — recheck).
