# Claims Policy — MyRhythm

**Version 1 · Binding on every asset: app copy, marketing, decks, emails, investor material, research output.**

## Positioning frame

**"After rehab, before routine."**

MyRhythm is not better than rehab and does not compete with it. It is the missing
follow-through stage — the period where functional scores look fine on paper but the person
is still relearning who they are and how to trust their own mind.

## Permitted claim domains

We may claim change in, and gather evidence about:

1. **Confidence** — feeling able to rely on your own plan.
2. **Identity** — recognising yourself again, re-forming a sense of who you are.
3. **Behaviour** — capturing, committing, returning, rescheduling, involving people.
4. **Quality of life** — days that hold together, less friction, less carer load.

These are precisely the domains that clinical rehab standards admit they do not cover.

## Forbidden territory

Never claim, imply or measure:

- Improvement in FIM scores, cognitive function, memory capacity or neurological recovery
- Diagnosis, treatment, prevention or management of any condition
- Reduction in readmission, relapse, seizures, falls or any medical event
- Medication effects, therapeutic equivalence, or being a substitute for clinical care
- "Clinically proven", "evidence-based treatment", "rehabilitation programme", "therapy"

Also banned externally (product-category memory): "brain OS", "operating system for your
brain", "second brain", "brain gym".

## Safe substitutions

| Do not write | Write instead |
| --- | --- |
| Improves cognitive function | Helps daily life hold together |
| Clinically proven | Built with, and used by, people living it |
| Treatment plan | Life-ready plan |
| Patient | Person, member |
| Reduces readmission | Supports follow-through after discharge |
| Boosts memory | Captures what was said so you don't have to hold it |

## Outreach script (approved)

> "I work with women in the months after their neuro rehab episode ends — where the
> functional scores look good on paper, but she's still relearning who she is and how to
> trust her own mind. I'd love fifteen minutes to explore whether that's a gap your
> discharged patients experience too."

## Pre-publish checklist

Before any asset ships, confirm all six:

- [ ] No clinical outcome verb (improve / treat / reduce / cure / prevent) applied to a medical noun
- [ ] Every claim sits in Confidence, Identity, Behaviour or Quality of Life
- [ ] Positioned as the stage **after** rehab, never as an alternative to it
- [ ] Disclaimer present where an outcome is described: MyRhythm does not diagnose, treat or fix conditions
- [ ] Any number quoted is traceable to `/founder/evidence` or a cited source
- [ ] Any research figure passes the k=20 floor

## Ownership

The founder is the final approver. Any asset failing the checklist does not ship, regardless
of deadline.
