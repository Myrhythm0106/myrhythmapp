## Draft 2 — Tue 15 Sept · "#IChoose one clear next step"

Good intentions are not a plan.

I used to leave appointments with a list: drink more water, walk daily, call the GP, sort the medication. Noble list. Completely useless. Nothing was dated, nothing reminded me. By day three the paper was in a drawer.

Now I work in ones. One step. One owner. One date.

**#IChoose one clear next step.**

What's the one step you're actually taking this week — not the list, the one?

## Draft 3 — Wed 16 Sept · "#IChoose to honour my own pace"

My pace is mine. Some days it's slow and that's not failure; it's information.

**#IChoose to honour my own pace.**
