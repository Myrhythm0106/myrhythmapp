## The One Ask Rule

Every post, talk, email and video ends with exactly one of:

- "Get the #IChoose 365 journal."
- "Join the MyRhythm founding list."

If a piece of content ends with neither, it is entertainment, not marketing.

## App-proof hours cap

- **Maximum:** two fixed blocks per week.
- **Allowed work:** device capture test, OAuth key install, Support Circle permission test, action-accuracy check.
- **Not allowed:** new features, redesigns, copy experiments, "just one more thing."

If the app work is not on the allowed list, it is parked in the backlog at the bottom of this file.

---

## Claims guardrail

Allowed words: confidence, identity, follow-through, quality of life, choice, rhythm, support, next step, plan, diary, traceability.

Forbidden words and phrases: cure, treat, fix, recovery outcome, medical result, clinical improvement, brain training, any named diagnosis promise.

Before publishing anything, ask: "Am I promising a health outcome?" If yes, rewrite.
