## The rules that keep this working

1. **One Ask Rule:** every post, talk and email ends with exactly one of "get the journal" or "join the founding list".
2. **Two platforms only** until December: LinkedIn primary + one of your choosing.
3. **Twenty minutes a day** of content. Thirty minutes on Sunday.
4. **App hours capped** at two blocks a week, proof items only. No new features.
5. **Claims guardrail:** confidence, identity, follow-through, quality of life. Never a health outcome. Check against `docs/claims-policy.md` before anything ships.
6. **Hard gate:** Founding Member seats open only after every box in `docs/app-proof-checklist.md` is ticked.
