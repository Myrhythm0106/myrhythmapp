# MyRhythm — Honest readiness statement

_Last reviewed with the build described below._

## The plain answer

**MyRhythm is NOT fully ready for real, unrestricted use by other people.**

It is ready for a small, hand-held founders group who know they are testing, who have been
briefed, and who can tell us when something breaks. It is not ready for strangers, for
clinicians relying on it, or for paying customers.

## What genuinely works today

- Capture → write-up → next steps → diary → reminders, end to end.
- Two-tap recording, with recovery if a capture is interrupted.
- Independent retention: the audio, the write-up/transcript and the next steps are each
  kept or released separately.
- Searchable diary of conversations, completed steps and brain-health snapshots.
- Brain-health snapshot with score, answers and trend over repeat runs.
- Support Circle invitations, permissions and encouragement notes.
- Calendar: subscribe to any calendar that publishes a link (Apple/iCloud, Fastmail and
  most work calendars) so busy times are respected.

## What is not ready, and why

| Blocker | Why it matters | What clears it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google and Outlook connection keys are not installed | Those two buttons cannot complete a connection | Add the OAuth credentials for each provider |
| Payments are in test mode | No real money can be taken | Switch Stripe to live once pricing is final |
| Long recordings unproven on real phones | A 2–4 hour capture on a real iPhone/Android is untested | A device test session on both platforms |
| No independent accessibility or security review | Primary audience includes people with memory and cognitive challenges | Book an external review |
| Support Circle permissions untested across two real accounts | Permission leaks would be serious | A two-account permission test |

## What to say to a tester

"This is an early Founding Edition. Everything you capture is real and yours, but treat it
as a companion, not the only copy. Tell me the moment something feels confusing — that's the
point of you being here."
