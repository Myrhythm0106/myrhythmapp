# MyRhythm — Scenarios, customers and pain points

A talking sheet for conversations with individuals, families, employers and rehab teams.
Language rule: confidence, identity, behaviour, quality of life. No clinical outcome claims.

**One line:** MyRhythm is the app that keeps your plan going after the appointment ends.

---

## 1. The person discharged from neuro rehab

**Pain:** The appointments stop, the plan lives on a folded sheet of paper, and nobody is
checking in. Functional scores looked fine; life doesn't feel fine.

**What MyRhythm does:** Records the discharge or follow-up conversation, turns it into a
short list of next steps with owners and dates, puts them in the diary, and reminds at the
right level. Every step traces back to the conversation it came from.

**What they say afterwards:** "I stopped losing what people told me."

## 2. The family member in the room

**Pain:** They become the memory for two people, and they burn out doing it.

**What MyRhythm does:** Support Circle. They see only what they've been given permission to
see, can leave encouragement on a step, and can be looped into a specific action by email —
without needing their own subscription for the person's data.

## 3. The rehab or community team

**Pain:** Continuity after discharge is the weakest link in the pathway, and it is hard to
evidence what happened once the patient walked out.

**What MyRhythm does:** A 30/60/90-day continuity report the person chooses to share, with
reference codes tying each step to its source conversation. Supports the follow-through
story CQC, CARF and UKROC-style reporting expects teams to tell.

## 4. The professional with ADHD or long-covid brain fog

**Pain:** Meetings happen, actions evaporate. Productivity tools assume an ideal brain.

**What MyRhythm does:** Two taps to capture, structured next steps, RACI in everyday words,
scheduling that respects when their brain is actually sharp — and lets them override it when
the client is only free at 4pm.

## 5. The carer of someone with dementia or MCI

**Pain:** Appointments come fast, details are lost, and the same questions get asked again.

**What MyRhythm does:** A searchable diary of every conversation, write-up and brain-health
snapshot in date order. Nothing is lost even after the audio is deleted.

## 6. The employer or occupational health lead

**Pain:** A valued employee returns after a health event and quietly struggles; adjustments
are guesswork.

**What MyRhythm does:** The person owns their data and shares only what they choose. Their
own rhythm data helps them plan demanding work into hours that work for them.

## Objections and honest answers

| Objection | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| "Is this a medical device?" | No. It does not diagnose, treat or fix anything. It is a productivity and follow-through companion. |
| "Who owns the recordings?" | The person does. They choose how long audio, the write-up and the steps are each kept — separately. |
| "Isn't this just a notes app?" | A notes app gives you text. MyRhythm gives you owned, dated, reminded next steps traceable to the conversation. |
| "Will it overwhelm someone with memory problems?" | Three choices maximum per screen, one primary action, large targets, first-person language. |
