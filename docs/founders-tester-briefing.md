# MyRhythm — Founders Group Briefing (one page)

**What this is:** an early Founding Edition of MyRhythm, the app that keeps your plans and actions going after an appointment or conversation ends. You are one of 5–15 people helping shape it.

**What it is not:** a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or fix any condition.

## Please try these (about 20 minutes)
1. Open https://myrhythmapp.com, tap **Get Started** and create your account.
2. Answer the questions and read your snapshot. Tap **Keep these** on your top 3.
3. Save one real thing you need to remember this week.
4. Record or note one real conversation in Memory Bridge and check the next steps it finds.
5. Come back tomorrow and notice the daily welcome.

## Please tell us
- Where did you hesitate, feel confused or feel overwhelmed?
- Did anything feel wrong, invented or out of place?
- What one moment made you think "I need this"?
- Would you keep using it? Why or why not?

## Good to know
- Treat it as a companion, not the only copy of anything important.
- Payments are not live. Nothing will be charged.
- Report anything odd straight away — that is the point of you being here.

## Founder scripts (10 minutes each)

**Real phone test (iPhone, then Android):** sign in → Memory Bridge → record for 1–2 hours → Save & Extract → check the capture shows the right length, steps appear, and it survives closing and reopening the app. Tick item 1/2 in `app-proof-checklist.md`.

**Two-account Support Circle test:** from your account, invite the second account to one capture only → sign in as them → confirm they see that capture and nothing else → remove the permission → confirm it disappears. Tick item 3.
