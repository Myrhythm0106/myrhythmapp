# Founders-Market Marketing Plan — v1

**Status:** Living document · v1 · Anchor date 28 July 2026
**Owner:** Founder (only)
**Companion docs:** [`problem-fit-and-market.md`](./problem-fit-and-market.md) · [`market-evidence.md`](./market-evidence.md) · [`feature-value-ranking.md`](./feature-value-ranking.md) · [`investor-loom-script.md`](./investor-loom-script.md) · [`investor-loom-distribution.md`](./investor-loom-distribution.md) · [`discharge-bridge-kit.md`](./discharge-bridge-kit.md) · [`90-day-sprint.md`](./90-day-sprint.md)

> **What this doc is.** The **operational, week-by-week action plan** for putting the MyRhythm MVP in front of the founder's immediate warm network — friends, family, ex-colleagues, brain-injury community contacts, clinicians, and other founders — during the Founding phase.
>
> **What this doc is not.** Not a public launch plan. Not a paid-acquisition plan. Not a content/SEO plan. Not a press plan. Those come after 3 signed clinical LOIs.

---

## 1 · Objective

**By 1 October 2026:**
- 100 paying Founding Members (£10/mo, price-locked)
- 3 signed clinical Letters of Intent (rehab centre, NHS trust, or ABI charity)
- ≥40% weekly-active rate among existing Founding Members

**One primary CTA everywhere:** *"Become a Founding Member"* → `/subscribe`. No competing CTAs during the Founding phase.

**Why these targets.** 100 members is the smallest sample size that produces a credible retention curve for the pre-seed conversation. 3 LOIs is the smallest number that makes "clinical distribution channel" defensible in an investor room. Both are hand-reachable through the four audience rings below — no advertising required.

## 2 · Audiences — four concentric rings

Every outreach action targets exactly one ring. Do not mix rings in one message; the framing is different for each.

### Ring 1 — Personal warm list
The people who already know the founder story.
- Family, close friends, godparents, in-laws
- Ex-colleagues and former direct reports
- People who checked in on the founder's family during recovery
- Church, gym, book-club, school-gate contacts

**Target size:** 80–120 named contacts. **Ask:** try the app, become a Founding Member, share with one person like you.

### Ring 2 — Brain-injury community contacts
People inside the survivor / carer / clinician ecosystem the founder has already met.
- Headway UK local groups (regional coordinators)
- ABI online communities (peer contacts, not cold posts)
- Fellow survivor-founders and survivor-writers
- Carers UK contacts
- Rehab-alumni WhatsApp groups the founder is already in

**Target size:** 30–50 named contacts. **Ask:** try the app, share with one survivor/carer in your circle, tell us what breaks.

### Ring 3 — Clinicians / rehab contacts
People who can sign an LOI, not people who will become paying members themselves.
- The rehab team involved in the founder's family case
- Consultants and OTs met through Headway
- Rehab centres already flagged in `docs/discharge-bridge-kit.md`
- One or two friendly NHS commissioners

**Target size:** 8–12 named contacts. **Ask:** 25-min call, review the Discharge Bridge Kit, sign an LOI.

### Ring 4 — Founder-of-founders
Other early-stage founders, angels, advisors — not for direct sales, for **warm referrals** into Rings 1–3.
- Founders in adjacent spaces (health-tech, wellness, care-tech)
- Angels who've backed brain-health or care-tech before
- Advisors the founder has met at events / on calls

**Target size:** 15–25 named contacts. **Ask:** one intro per person, into any of the other three rings.

## 5 · 12-week rolling action plan (3 Aug → 26 Oct 2026)

**Time budget:** ~4 hours of marketing work per week. Anything over that will not survive the sprint.

Weekly rhythm (every Monday, 20 min, absorbed into the existing `90-day-sprint.md` §8 review):
1. Log last week's five metrics (see §8).
2. Choose this week's outreach targets from the contact spreadsheet.
3. Draft this week's emails in one sitting (Monday afternoon).
4. Send Tuesday–Thursday.
5. Reply-triage on Friday.

### Week grid

| Week | Dates | Outreach target | Content task (max 1) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3–9 Aug | Ring 1: 20 emails | Record + upload Loom | Personal-story framing; ask people to try /subscribe |
| 2 | 10–16 Aug | Ring 1: 20 emails · Ring 4: 5 emails | — | Follow up on Week 1 non-replies (one nudge only) |
| 3 | 17–23 Aug | Ring 2: 10 emails · Ring 4: 5 emails | Monthly Update #1 | First Monday of Aug behaviour; short and honest |
| 4 | 24–30 Aug | Ring 2: 10 emails · Ring 3: 3 emails (clinician) | — | First Bridge Kit hand-off meeting target |
| 5 | 31 Aug–6 Sep | Ring 1 re-touch (10) · Ring 3: 3 emails | — | Ask Ring 1 who they'd introduce (Ring 2 referrals) |
| 6 | 7–13 Sep | Warm referrals from Ring 4 (target 5) | Monthly Update #2 | Report Loom past-3:30 count, member count |
| 7 | 14–20 Sep | Ring 3: 3 clinician meetings booked | — | Aim for LOI #1 signed |
| 8 | 21–27 Sep | Ring 1 & 2 re-touch (15) | — | "We're close to 100 — want to be one of them?" |
| 9 | 28 Sep–4 Oct | Push for members #90–#100 | — | Personal thank-you notes to first 25 members |
| 10 | 5–11 Oct | Ring 3: LOI #2 & #3 push | Monthly Update #3 | Include first two LOIs (redacted) |
| 11 | 12–18 Oct | Retention week — 1:1 with 10 members | — | No new outreach; talk to the members you have |
| 12 | 19–26 Oct | Review vs targets. Plan next 12 weeks. | Write v2 of this plan | Only rewrite if a metric was missed 2+ weeks running |

**Slippage rule.** If two consecutive weeks miss targets, do NOT add more channels. Do a 30-min diagnosis: (a) is the ask wrong, (b) is the audience wrong, (c) is the product wrong? Fix one, don't fix all three at once.

## 7 · Monthly Founder Update template

Sent on the first Monday of each month to **every contact who has ever received an outreach email**, with a one-line opt-out at the bottom. Plain text. Under 250 words. One update, three sections.

> Subject: **MyRhythm — [Month] update**
>
> Hi —
>
> One short monthly note. Skip to the bottom to opt out; no offence taken.
>
> **What shipped.** [1–2 lines. Feature or improvement based on member feedback.]
>
> **What the members are telling us.** [1–2 lines. A real quote if possible, anonymised.]
>
> **Where we're going next month.** [1–2 lines. Honest, no over-promising.]
>
> Founding Member spots remaining: **[N of 100]** · £10/mo price-locked → [https://myrhythmapp.com/subscribe]
>
> If you'd like off this list, reply with the word "off" — I read every one.
>
> — [Founder]

**Rule:** never send a monthly update that has no shipped feature and no member quote. If both are missing that month, skip the update. Consistency of *substance* matters more than consistency of *cadence*.

## 9 · Guardrails — non-negotiable

Every outreach message must pass all six checks before sending:

1. **No medical claims.** MyRhythm does not diagnose, treat, cure, or fix any condition. Never say "helps with brain injury / ADHD / dementia" — say "designed with those things in mind" or name the survivor lens explicitly.
2. **No public broadcast during Founding phase.** No LinkedIn, X, TikTok, Instagram posts about MyRhythm. Personal 1:1 only.
3. **No paid acquisition until 3 LOIs signed.**
4. **Every email personal, from the founder's address.** No `team@`, no marketing platform, no template merge fields visible.
5. **Locked wording.** External: **Memory-First Design™** and **app** (never "OS", "brain OS", "second brain", "operating system"). Internal only: "Collaborative Cognitive Continuity layer running the 4C loop".
6. **One primary CTA.** *"Become a Founding Member"* → `/subscribe`. No competing "Book a demo" / "Start free trial" / "Download the whitepaper" asks in the same message.

## 10 · Maintenance model

- **Owner:** Founder. No delegate during v0.1.
- **Cadence:** Monday, 20 minutes, inside the existing `90-day-sprint.md` §8 review.
- **Update trigger:** monthly, OR when a metric target is missed two weeks running, OR when Founding Member count crosses a round number (25 / 50 / 75 / 100).
- **Version convention:** on material change, save `founders-market-marketing-plan_v2.md` alongside this one. Never overwrite v1 — comparison over time is the point.
- **Retirement trigger:** this plan retires when either (a) 100 Founding Members reached AND 3 LOIs signed, OR (b) 26 Oct 2026 reached, whichever comes first. Whatever replaces it is a **public-launch plan**, not this one.

## 11 · What this plan deliberately excludes

Listed here so future-founder doesn't wonder if they were forgotten. Each of these is a *decision*, not a *gap*.

- **SEO / content marketing.** Requires sustained cadence and analytics loop; solo founder can't hold it AND ship v0.2.
- **Paid ads (Meta / Google / TikTok).** Cannot buy trust in this category. Waste of capital pre-LOI.
- **Podcast tour / press.** Only credible after 3 LOIs. Founder time better spent on the LOI conversations directly.
- **Community platform (Discord / Slack).** Members expect responses; the founder cannot provide 7-day response cover during v0.1. Revisit at 250 members.
- **Referral program tech.** Ask verbally in every Ring 1 / Ring 2 email — that is the referral program until Month 6.
- **CRM / email automation.** A single spreadsheet + plain-text email out-performs tooling at this scale. Tooling gets bought when it saves >2 hrs/week.
- **A/B testing anything.** Sample sizes are too small to learn from. Pick a version, ship it, listen.

## 12 · The 45-second version

> MyRhythm has 12 weeks to reach 100 Founding Members and 3 clinical LOIs from a hand-shared, warm-network push. Four audience rings, three channels, one CTA, five metrics, one weekly log line, one founder. No ads, no press, no public posts. If the numbers work, this plan retires on 26 October and a public-launch plan replaces it. If they don't, the diagnosis is one of three things — wrong ask, wrong audience, wrong product — and we fix one at a time.

## 13 · Positioning, messaging and conversion (added 27 Aug 2026, per the approved 3-Phase Plan)

### 13.1 The single line everything hangs off

> **MyRhythm is the app that keeps your plan going after the appointment ends or the commitment has been made.**

- Category descriptor: **Memory-First Design™**.
- Hero feature in all external messaging: **Memory Bridge** (record → agreed next steps → shared with the people who matter). Decision approved in `strategic-documents/Memory-Bridge-Positioning-Decision.md`; the in-app 4C loop is unchanged.
- Three problems, in this order: the **Discharge Cliff**, the **Clinical-Ready vs Life-Ready Gap**, the **Ideal-Brain Assumption**.
- Feature language uses the 4Rs verbatim: **Remove / Reduce / Return / Reconnect**.
- Language discipline: confidence, identity, behaviour, quality of life. Never clinical outcome language; `docs/claims-policy.md` is binding on every asset.

### 13.2 Messaging by audience

| Audience | Their words for the problem | Our promise | Proof to show |
|---|---|---|---|
| Survivor / post-discharge | "They said I was ready. I don't feel ready." | Your plan keeps going after the appointment ends. | Discharge Bridge Kit, Memory Bridge capture |
| Carer / family | "I'm the only one holding it all." | No one walks alone — Reconnect. | Support Circle, shared next steps |
| ADHD / stress / focus | "I know what to do, I just don't follow through." | Follow-through that fits the day you're actually having. | Energy-matched schedule, reminder ladder |
| Clinician / rehab centre | "We lose sight of them at discharge." | Written evidence of follow-through after discharge. | 30/60/90-day continuity report, clinical export PDF |
| Investor / advisor | "Is there a real channel here?" | Consumer entry, clinical distribution. | Loom, data room, LOIs |

### 13.3 Conversion path — one CTA, no forks

```text
Warm email  →  Landing page  →  Become a Founding Member  →  /subscribe
                                                              ↓
                              Access code (testers) or Stripe checkout
                                                              ↓
                    Assessment  →  /launch/home  →  first Memory Bridge capture
```

**Activation moment:** first capture that produces at least one scheduled next step. A member who reaches it is *activated*; a member who doesn't gets a personal follow-up from the founder. Target: ≥60% of new members activated within 7 days.

### 13.4 Eight-week outreach cadence

| Week | Ring | Action | Weekly target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | Ring 1 — personal warm list | Personal emails: try it, become a Founding Member, pass to one person like you | 10 sends |
| 4 | Ring 1 + Ring 2 — brain-injury community | Ask each contact for one survivor or carer tester | 10 sends |
| 5 | Ring 3 — clinicians / rehab | 25-min calls, Bridge Kit handout, LOI ask | 4 calls booked |
| 6 | Ring 3 continued | Follow-up, continuity report walkthrough, LOI sent | 2 LOIs out |
| 7 | Ring 4 — founders / advisors | One warm intro each, into Rings 1–3 | 8 asks |
| 8 | All rings | Second-touch on non-repliers, first Founder Update | 30 touches |

### 13.5 Metrics reviewed every Monday

Logged as one line per week in `docs/90-day-sprint.md` §8 (format defined there):

- Sends and replies by ring (targets: warm ≥40%, cold ≥10%, clinician ≥60%).
- Loom viewers past 3:30 — the qualified-lead signal.
- Sign-ups · activated · weekly-active.
- Founding Member seats against the 500 cap; Friends & Family against the 50 cap.
- LOI funnel: contacted → call → kit sent → signed.
